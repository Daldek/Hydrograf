# Plan: Nogat's GUI kit in Hydrograf, the app menu and the hand-off

Approved: 2026-11-12, the author, as step 5 of Nogat's plan
`docs/plans/2026-11-12-gui-links-and-handoff.md` (stage 1 of the GUI platform, ECO-058:
„przejdź do etapu 1”); the details by delegated authority, decided in ADR-082.

Requirements: Nogat ECO-058, DEV-15.4 (the app menu), DEV-15.5 (the hand-off), DEV-15.6
(copies byte for byte), DEV-18.1 (the table `gui` of `[nogat]`); Nogat's kit 0.1.0
(`design/kit/` at Nogat `7786271`); ADR-065, ADR-068, ADR-080.

## Goal

From Hydrograf's top bar a person reaches the other GUIs that the deployment runs, in one
click („Aplikacje”), and the hand-off to Hydrolog is Nogat's code, so its labels, file
name, text and clipboard behave as in every GUI of the ecosystem. Hydrograf's copies of
Nogat's files are current.

## Decisions (ADR-082)

1. The deployment's table `gui` is the JSON object of `HYDROGRAF_GUI`, passed by
   `docker-compose.yml` to the API, checked when the settings load and served by
   `GET /api/gui`; without it `{}` and no menu.
2. One ES module of Hydrograf's own, `js/nogat.js`, on `/`, `/admin` and `/instrukcja/`:
   it imports the kit, gives `<nogat-app-menu current="hydrograf">` (top bar, left of the
   theme switch) the table and puts the hand-off module on `window.Hydrograf.nogat`.
3. `js/handoff.js` keeps the API's documents and takes the labels, the copy with its
   fallback field, the download and the statuses from `nogat-handoff.js`; the status links
   „Otwórz Hydrologa” when the table names Hydrolog.
4. `scripts/bump_asset_versions.py` versions the imports of an own ES module as well.
5. The copies: the kit and the marks of the four GUIs in `frontend/nogat/`, `tokens.css`
   and `schemas/nogat/error_registry.json` of Nogat `7786271` (`core/_errors.py`
   generated again); `handoff-1.schema.json` and `schemas/agent/` are current already.
